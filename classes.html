Coursework

A selection of courses that have shaped my understanding of aerospace engineering, mathematics and control systems. Only the most relevant courses are listed here.

Penn State (First Year)

Linear Algebra

Fall 2023

Introduces vector and matrix algebra and the solution of systems of linear equations. Studies linear independence, bases and orthonormal bases, rank, linear transformations and diagonalisation. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors are emphasised, and concepts are implemented using contemporary software.

Electricity & Magnetism

Spring 2024

Calculus-based introduction to classical electricity and magnetism, including electric charge and electric fields, Gauss's law, electric potential, capacitance, current, resistance and circuits, magnetic fields and fields due to currents, induction and inductance, magnetism of matter, Maxwell's equations and electromagnetic oscillations.

Capstone Project

Spring 2023

Led a team project tackling a real-world engineering challenge. We defined requirements, designed a prototype and iterated through testing and refinement. The project strengthened skills in project management, collaboration and technical communication.

Virginia Tech (Sophomore Year)

Electronics for AOE

Fall 2024

Covers electrical circuits with discrete passive and active components. Uses phasors and impedance to analyse AC power systems and introduces digital electronics. Emphasises electronics for autonomous and piloted aerospace and ocean vehicles, including instrumentation and data acquisition for navigation, guidance and control.

Computational Methods

Fall 2024

Solves engineering problems with numerical methods and software. Topics include truncation and round-off error, root finding, interpolation, spline and polynomial regression, numerical integration and differentiation, and solution of linear systems of equations.

Statics & Structures

Spring 2025

Studies vector mechanics of forces and moments, free-body diagrams, couples, resultants and equilibrium of particles and rigid bodies. Covers trusses and frames, centroids, area moments and the parallel-axis theorem. Analyses distributed loads, internal axial and shear forces and bending moments, constructs shear and moment diagrams, and examines stress and strain in bars and beams.

Dynamics

Spring 2025

Covers kinematics and kinetics of particles and rigid bodies using Newton's laws of motion. Discusses work and energy, impulse and momentum methods, mass moments of inertia and rotating axes, and planar and three-dimensional motion.

Thin-Walled Structures

Spring 2025

Examines stringer-stiffened thin-walled structures and their basic elements. Analyses forces, moments, stresses and deformations in beams, including flexure stress and deflection. Studies plane of bending and plane of loading for beams with asymmetric cross sections, torsion of thin-walled members, shear flow and shear centre in stiffened panels, and multi-cell beams.

Virginia Tech (Junior Year – In Progress)

Fluid Dynamics for AOE

Fall 2025

Introduces fundamentals of fluid mechanics including stress, statics, viscosity and both laminar and turbulent flow. Covers conservation of mass and momentum, vorticity, circulation and lift. Applies Navier-Stokes equations to ideal two-dimensional flow using streamlines, stream functions and velocity potentials, and discusses thin airfoil theory. Explores boundary layer physics, transition and the boundary layer equations with computational tools.

System Dynamics & Control

Fall 2025

Analyzes free and forced response of linear time-invariant systems in frequency and time domains. Models low-order mechanical systems and studies transmission and absorption of vibrations. Discusses transient and steady-state performance specifications and introduces closed-loop control using PID feedback with root-locus stability analysis.

Aerospace Structures

Fall 2025

Investigates load paths in aircraft, stress transformation, buckling and plate bending. Provides tools for sizing components under combined loads.

Astromechanics

Fall 2025

Examines orbital mechanics including the two-body problem, orbit determination and interplanetary transfers.

Nonlinear Dynamics & Chaos

Fall 2025

Studies phase-plane analysis, limit cycles, bifurcations and chaotic behaviour. Highlights how linear control tools must be extended for complex systems.
