Undergraduate Research Presentation

Virginia Tech GNC Lab • 2025

I delivered a seminar on my undergraduate research in guidance, navigation and control (GNC). The talk focused on building and testing a three‑degree‑of‑freedom thrust‑stand testbed and applying genetic algorithms for PID tuning. Sharing these findings honed my ability to distil complex engineering into an understandable story.

Key Contributions

  • Developed slides explaining the physics of UAV dynamics and control architectures.
  • Demonstrated how genetic algorithms can automate controller tuning and compared results to classical PID tuning methods.
  • Engaged with questions from peers and faculty, strengthening my conceptual grasp and communication skills.

Skills & Tools

  • Public speaking & technical storytelling
  • Genetic algorithms & control theory
  • Presentation design (LaTeX, PowerPoint)
