GoAERO Team Lead & Preliminary Design Review

GoAERO Heavy‑Lift UAV Competition • Aug 2024 – Sep 2025

Serving as avionics and programming lead for Virginia Tech’s heavy‑lift UAV team, I oversaw integration of sensors, flight controllers and telemetry for a competition‑grade aircraft. Our team competed in the GoAERO Prize, a NASA‑sponsored challenge to build next‑generation cargo drones. I orchestrated the ROS2‑based communication architecture, ensuring robust data links between onboard computers and ground control. In preparation for the Preliminary Design Review (PDR) I authored a comprehensive report detailing avionics layouts, flight‑control algorithms and the communication stack.

Key Contributions

  • Coordinated a cross‑disciplinary team to select sensors and avionics hardware for a 20 kg payload UAV.
  • Designed and implemented a modular ROS2 network for flight‑control, navigation and payload communication.
  • Produced the PDR package, including system schematics, requirement traceability and risk analysis.
  • Guided software integration and troubleshooting, helping the team win the NASA University Innovation prize.

Skills & Tools

  • ROS2 and C++ development
  • System engineering & documentation
  • Flight‑control architecture design
