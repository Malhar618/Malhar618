GoAERO Team Lead & Preliminary Design Review

GoAERO Heavy-Lift UAV Competition • Aug 2024 – Sep 2025

As avionics and programming lead for Virginia Tech's heavy-lift UAV team, I oversaw ROS 2 messaging, interfaces and integration between the flight stack, sensors and onboard compute for a competition-grade aircraft. Our team competed in the GoAERO Prize, a NASA-sponsored challenge to build next-generation cargo drones. I built the SITL/HITL and telemetry pipeline for flight-test readiness and authored the Preliminary Design Review (PDR) report covering avionics layouts, flight-control algorithms and the communication stack. The team won the NASA University Innovation Prize ($30K).

Key Contributions

  • Coordinated a cross-disciplinary team to select sensors and avionics hardware for a 20 kg payload UAV.
  • Designed and implemented a modular ROS 2 network for flight-control, navigation and payload communication.
  • Built SITL/HITL + telemetry/command pipeline to de-risk integration and support flight-test readiness.
  • Produced the PDR package, including system schematics, requirement traceability and risk analysis.
  • Won the NASA University Innovation Prize ($30K) as part of a multidisciplinary team.
