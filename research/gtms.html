GTMS Internship

Graduate Traction & Mobility Systems • 2025

As part of the GTMS research program, I investigated cooling systems and traction‑control algorithms for next‑generation heavy machinery. My work combined finite‑element analysis with model‑based control to ensure high‑performance and reliability under extreme operating conditions.

Key Contributions

  • Performed finite‑element simulations of engine and battery cooling modules to optimise heat dissipation and structural integrity.
  • Developed preliminary control algorithms in MATLAB/Simulink for traction and braking systems; translated prototypes into C++ for embedded deployment.
  • Collaborated with mechanical and electrical engineers to integrate sensor feedback into closed‑loop control models.
  • Documented research findings and presented recommendations to senior engineers and academic advisors.

Skills & Tools

  • Finite‑element analysis (NX, NASTRAN)
  • Model‑based control design (MATLAB/Simulink)
  • C++ & Python programming
  • Data analysis & reporting
