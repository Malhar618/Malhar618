Two Caterpillar rotations gave me both sides of engineering judgment: physics-backed analysis in GTMS/LPSD and model-based control development in Caterpillar Technology.

Embedded Controls Co-op · Caterpillar Technology

In my current co-op, I work on modular Simulink controls for traction control, ABS, and vehicle-stability functions. The work centers on making control components reusable across different actuation paths: brake based, clutch based, electric motor, and related machine configurations.

  • Architected situational speed target generators and wheel-level control logic for scenario-specific traction behavior.
  • Worked on machine-state estimator interfaces, filters, and error conventions so a modular wheel-speed generator can serve multiple vehicle-control systems.
  • Developed multi-rate sensing integration concepts for IMU and wheel-speed streams with different sampling rates.
  • Used Dynasty plant models to test model behavior, reduce oscillations seen in data, and account for actuator physical constraints.
  • Generated embedded C from Simulink for early ECM timing and sampling-rate feasibility checks.

GTMS Simulation Internship · LPSD

In summer 2025, I supported Global Thermal Management Systems design decisions through structural, thermal, and CFD simulation work on cooling products. The strongest engineering lesson was how solver setup, boundary conditions, documentation, and cross-functional reviews control whether simulation results are useful.

  • Performed finite-element analysis on cooling-system components and lifting/operational load cases.
  • Compared NX Nastran workflows against legacy solver approaches and documented setup techniques.
  • Helped accelerate adoption of NX Nastran workflows across the group.
  • Participated in structural reviews and cross-functional engineering meetings.
