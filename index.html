About Me

I'm a third-year Aerospace Engineering student at Virginia Tech concentrating in Guidance, Control & Navigation. I thrive at the intersection of math, physics and software, whether I'm developing embedded traction controllers at Caterpillar or writing MATLAB and C++ code for UAV control loops. My work is grounded in real projects: embedded controls co-op at Caterpillar, a 3-DOF thrust stand for the U.S. Navy, and UWB-based collaborative navigation research in the AVA Lab (NAViTi).

Outside of engineering, I enjoy hiking, exploring new cities and mentoring other students in robotics. Building drones from the ground up keeps me curious and connects abstract theory to tangible results.
