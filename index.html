Intervehicle UWB ranging for GNSS-degraded navigation

  • Built MATLAB logging/parsing tools for distance-only payloads and relative-pose simulation geometry.

Controls testbed

Three-DOF thrust stand for UAV controller tuning

Undergraduate research with Dr. L'Afflitto on a NAVAIR-funded thrust-stand system for small UAVs, integrating Pixhawk 6C, Odroid M1S, ROS 2 Galactic, Dynamixel actuation, and MATLAB genetic-algorithm workflows for inner-loop tuning.

  • Implemented C++ ROS 2 nodes for Dynamixel moment cancellation using rigid-body inertia models.
  • Worked from CAD and PyChrono dynamics models to understand inertia and motor constraints.
  • Presented the research at the AirTalent Symposium and documented setup, wiring, data logging, GA workflow, and troubleshooting for future lab teams.
