About Me

I'm a third‑year Aerospace Engineering student at Virginia Tech concentrating in Guidance, Control & Navigation. I thrive at the intersection of math, physics and software, whether I'm running finite‑element simulations on heavy equipment or writing C++ and MATLAB code for UAV control loops. My academic work is grounded in real projects—I've tested traction controllers at Caterpillar, built a three‑degree‑of‑freedom thrust stand for the U.S. Navy and lead avionics development for a heavy‑lift UAV.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy hiking, exploring new cities and mentoring other students in robotics. Building drones from the ground up keeps me curious and connects abstract theory to tangible results.
