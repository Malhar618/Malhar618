Selected work

CARNATIONS Lab · Dr. Joerger

UWB collaborative navigation

Undergraduate research in Dr. Mathieu Joerger's group, part of the CARNATIONS center for resilient positioning, navigation, and timing. I work on UWB ranging between vehicles so they can localize each other when GNSS is degraded or untrusted.

  • Implemented a 3-link relative-pose estimator (candidate roots + WLS/MAP prior) reinforced by GPS; resolved 81/81 GPS-aligned vehicle frames with 5.99 cm mean fit RMSE.
  • Calibrated SS-TWR ranging across 7,553 outdoor samples (1.65–30.39 m), cutting leave-one-distance-out RMSE from 8.9 cm to 3.7 cm.
  • Extended COTS UWB range from 30 m to 100 m by rewriting DWM1001 firmware for a lower bit rate and longer preamble.

ACSL · NAVAIR Fellowship

Three-DOF thrust stand for UAV controller tuning

Research in Dr. L'Afflitto's Advanced Control Systems Lab under a NAVAIR Fellowship. The stand holds a small UAV in roll, pitch, and yaw so controllers can be tuned on hardware without flight risk.

  • Wrote C++ ROS 2 nodes for Dynamixel-actuated moment cancellation using rigid-body inertia and moment models, on a Pixhawk 6C + Odroid M1S running ROS 2 Galactic.
  • Helped drive the redesign that cut stand mass 28.7% (19.67 to 14.02 kg) and lowered the yaw torque requirement from 10.59 to 7.13 N·m, fixing motor stalls.
  • Implemented the MATLAB genetic-algorithm workflow for inner-loop PID tuning and performance-index evaluation.
